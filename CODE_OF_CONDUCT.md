# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all.
We expect our community to behave respectfully and professionally at all times.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Welcome diverse perspectives.
- Focus on constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Exclusionary behavior.
- Disrespect or verbal abuse.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by
opening an issue or contacting the maintainers directly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, both online and offline,
as well as in one-on-one communications.
